# Policy and Privacy

## Live Verification Protocol

For the target venue and year:

1. Open the official venue author page.
2. Verify dates, submission type, length, template, anonymization, supplementary
   material, accessibility, ethics, concurrent submission, and AI-use rules.
3. Verify linked ACM or SIGCHI policy pages when the venue delegates a rule.
4. Record the exact page URL and access date.
5. Mark unclear or conflicting rules as unverified and recommend contacting the
   venue chairs.

Never copy a prior year's numbers into a current report without verification.

## Privacy Boundary

- Author-side coaching: process only material the user is authorized to share.
- Reviewer-side work: do not upload a confidential submission to a third-party AI
  service unless the applicable venue policy and service confidentiality terms
  explicitly allow it.
- Local scripts in this package read local files and make no network requests.
- Warn users that model/application data handling is controlled by the platform
  running the skill, not by this Markdown package.
- Keep private research infrastructure, generated artifacts, unpublished
  manuscripts, reviews, and rebuttals outside this public repository.
- Do not expose internal research services, endpoints, identities, or raw
  outputs through public documentation or releases.

## Official Starting Points

- Current CHI site: https://chi2027.acm.org/authors/papers/
- ACM publication policies: https://www.acm.org/publications/policies
- ACM peer-review FAQ: https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/peer-review-faq

These links are starting points, not cached policy claims.
