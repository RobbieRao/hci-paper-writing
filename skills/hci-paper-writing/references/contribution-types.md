# HCI Contribution Types

Choose the primary type before evaluating prose. Hybrid papers may have a
secondary type, but should not present every project activity as a contribution.

| Type | Core promise | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Empirical understanding | Explain people, practices, contexts, relationships, or interaction | Transparent sampling and method, credible analysis, situated findings, HCI implications |
| Artifact or interaction technique | Make a new interaction or design possibility tangible | Rationale, implementation or material detail, use context, appropriate evaluation or reflection |
| Experience prototype or installation | Create a legible new experience, encounter, or speculative proposition | Vivid artifact, interaction loop, setting, audience response, reflective interpretation |
| System, tool, or infrastructure | Enable a new human workflow or capability | System boundary, feasibility, deployment or study evidence, failure conditions |
| Methodology | Offer a new way to design, study, analyze, or evaluate | Reproducible procedure, comparison to prior methods, demonstration of value |
| Theory or framework | Improve how HCI explains, predicts, or analyzes interaction | Defined constructs, relationship to prior theory, application to evidence or cases |
| Dataset or benchmark | Supply a reusable foundation for future HCI research | Provenance, coverage, annotation, access, ethics, limitations, example use |
| Argument or vision | Ask the community to reconsider a consequential assumption | Evidence-backed reasoning, counterarguments, stakes, affected communities, ramifications |
| Replication or validation | Test the stability or boundary of prior knowledge | Justification, faithful comparison, changed conditions, meaning of convergence or divergence |

## Alignment Tests

- Does the title name or strongly imply the primary type?
- Does contribution 1 deliver the title's promise?
- Does every contribution change what HCI can understand, build, study, or debate?
- Does each contribution have a corresponding evidence section?
- Are models, tools, datasets, and studies described as means unless they create
  a distinct HCI contribution?
