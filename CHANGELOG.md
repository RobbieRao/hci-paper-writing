# Changelog

## 0.5.0 - 2026-10-26

- Updated CHI 2027 author-side review calibration against public guidance checked
  on 2026-10-26, including five assessment dimensions and A/ARR/RR/RRX/X labels.
- Added repair-feasibility analysis and a clearer account of reviewer
  disagreement about contributions, evidence, method standards, and repairs.
- Strengthened task-to-claim alignment, result consistency, comparison choice,
  and the distinction between generated outputs and repeated judgments.
- Added automated-judge calibration and source-fidelity checks for AI systems,
  plus selection, category, and inference checks for reviews and frameworks.
- Made criticism distinguish reporting gaps, validity problems, procedural
  concerns, and the additional evidence needed for broader claims.
- Documented the discrepancy between the general process page and the dedicated
  announcement that the AI-assisted ADR rubric report is not used in CHI 2027.
- Added three original synthetic calibration exercises. No confidential reviews
  were added to the package or used as evaluation labels for this update.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-08-13

- Added a bilingual CHI 2022–2026 trend atlas covering rapidly growing,
  persistent, volatile, and declining-share topics.
- Added confidence-calibrated CHI 2027 scenarios for agency, situated AI
  deployment, epistemic interaction, multi-party effects, embodied generation,
  longitudinal relationships, and accessibility.
- Added facet-aware closest-work analysis across problem, contribution,
  method, findings, and limitations, including explicit contrast cases.
- Added search-stability checks so one wording or language variant cannot
  silently become a literature-coverage or novelty conclusion.
- Added rules that separate topic growth from overall conference expansion and
  prevent trend signals from becoming acceptance predictions.
- Added a tracked-file release guard for credentials, private paths, external
  symlinks, large files, and common local artifact formats.
- Expanded the deterministic test suite from 15 to 22 tests.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-08-12

- Added DOCX input, optional anonymization warnings, local LaTeX dependency and
  citation-key checks, and strict CI behavior for deterministic defects.
- Added an ordered project lifecycle with recorded, non-skippable stage
  transitions and a cold-resume handoff file.
- Added source ownership, author-endorsement, permission, and evidence tracking
  for integrating chats, meetings, email, reviewer text, and notes.
- Separated contribution form, research area, research tradition, and venue;
  made secondary contribution forms carry their own evidence requirements.
- Added HCI-specific novelty, positioning, contribution-noun, and non-defensive
  writing protocols.
- Expanded the deterministic test suite from 8 to 15 tests.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-08-10

- Added a safe `.hci-paper/` workspace initializer with persistent claim,
  figure, reviewer-comment, revision, and policy ledgers.
- Added HCI-specific reviewer-panel and meta-review protocols that preserve
  disagreement and disclose simulated independence.
- Added a closed-loop rebuttal and revision protocol linking every response
  promise to a manuscript change and verification step.
- Extended the deterministic manuscript audit with reverse outlines, LaTeX
  figure/table integrity checks, and a versioned JSON schema.
- Expanded unit tests and documented the new workflows in English and Chinese.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-10

- Initial public release with contribution diagnosis, method-aware red-teaming,
  revision planning, local manuscript preflight, and bilingual documentation.
