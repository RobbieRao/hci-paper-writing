# Sources and acknowledgments

HCI Paper Coach is independently authored. No third-party source code or skill
files are vendored in this repository.

The project's development was informed by public ideas and workflows from:

- [SNL-UCSB/paper-writing-skill](https://github.com/SNL-UCSB/paper-writing-skill)
  (MIT): structured brainstorming, claim-first writing, revision pipelines, and
  figure-to-claim thinking.
- [CCFA-Skills](https://github.com/mikubaka88/CCFA-Skills) (MIT): project-state,
  role-separated review, integrity-audit, rebuttal-ledger, and compression
  workflow ideas. HCI Paper Coach implements its own HCI-specific protocols and
  code.
- [academic-writing-skills](https://github.com/WenyuChiou/academic-writing-skills)
  (MIT): persistent paper context, claim and figure inventories, and multi-pass
  consistency-checking ideas.
- [ai-peer-review-skill](https://github.com/AlexWortega/ai-peer-review-skill)
  (MIT): collecting independent reviewer reports before meta-review and using
  stable machine-readable concern records.
- [scipilot-figure-skill](https://github.com/Haojae/scipilot-figure-skill)
  (MIT): separating deterministic figure checks from semantic and perceptual
  review. No plotting or inspection code is copied into this repository.
- [NousResearch/hermes-agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent)
  (MIT): claim-driven research and human-evaluation reporting workflows.
- [Auto-Research-In-Sleep](https://github.com/wanshuiyin/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep):
  claim-evidence and iterative review workflow ideas. No files are copied or
  redistributed here.
- Public HCI writing and review guidance from ACM, SIGCHI, CHI, CSCW, DIS, and
  UIST venue websites.
- [Guidelines for Reporting and Reviewing LLM-Integrated Systems in HCI](https://ianarawjo.github.io/Guidelines-for-Reporting-LLM-Integrated-Systems-in-HCI/),
  used as a starting point for the LLM-systems review lens.

Public discovery sources referenced by the evidence-grounding and benchmark
roadmap include the official ACM/SIGCHI CHI proceedings and author guidance,
[DBLP's CHI index](https://dblp.org/db/conf/chi/index), the Semantic Scholar Open
Research Corpus, [OpenAlex](https://docs.openalex.org/),
[PeerRead](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.09635), and OpenReview-derived datasets.
These are links and discovery starting points only: their content is not vendored
here, and each source retains its own license, terms, and coverage limitations.

During the 0.2 workflow review, we also inspected projects under noncommercial
licenses and projects without an identifiable license. Their code, templates,
and prose were deliberately excluded from this MIT project. High-level ideas
that are common to research workflow design were independently reimplemented;
no CC BY-NC or unlicensed files are redistributed here.

Official venue names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Reference
to a venue describes intended compatibility and does not imply endorsement.
